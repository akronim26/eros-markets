Scope: Persuade. Audience: perp traders and prediction-market users meeting Eros for the first time.
Action: open the terminal. Proof: the live testnet market read from chain, the spec's worked payoff,

category landing of claims, logos and invented throughput numbers.

OWN-WORLD: The pinned v0 brutalist system in Eros colours: Ivory #F2F1EC dot-grid ground, Ink #0A0A0B
1px frames and inverted panels, Signal #FF5A36 arrow squares, status squares and the mark; Geist Pixel
Square display, Geist Mono everything else; `// SECTION: NAME — 00n` rules; zero radius.

STORY: Read the promise (price, trade, resolve), watch the live market type itself out, understand
leverage and reserve cover from the payoff figure, see how outcomes are decided, then open the terminal.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Framed nav; pixel "PRICE. TRADE." headline; lifecycle node diagram (question,
probability, position → mark → halt, resolve, claim); "RESOLVE."; one paragraph; arrow-square CTA
"Open the terminal" plus "How it resolves".

TERMINAL.SYS panel types the real chain state of the market.
