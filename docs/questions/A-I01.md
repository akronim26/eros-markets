# A-I01: vault-level fee escrows (implementation choices to confirm on review)

Status: implemented on `integration/risk` by 0xr10t on 2026-10-02 and pending teammate review. The
change affects economic behavior (custody and fee classification), so the teammate who did not
write it reviews it and refreshes the fingerprints.

Spec basis (`docs/spec/risk_spec.md`, "Fee escrow and exceptional recovery"):
- Before the reserve residual is assigned, move exact `protocolFeeQ` and `keeperPayableQ` into
  separately recognized global vault fee escrows by internal bookkeeping.
- Reduce `allocationQ` by exactly those Q, with no rounded token transfer.
- Pay floor atoms on withdrawal. Fractions stay liabilities that cannot reach LP residual.
- Market allocation may become fractional. Aggregate custody stays exact.

The spec fixes all of the above. The code also makes six choices that the spec leaves open; each
is listed below so the reviewer can confirm or reject it.

1. **When reclassification happens:** at payout-scan completion (`PayoutLedger._scanPayoutPage`),
   before `_assessAvailable`.
   - At that point the snapshot is final and the settlement price is known.
   - It comes before the recovery assessment, the payout allocation and the reserve residual.
   - Fees are therefore senior to trader claims, as the spec's `RECOVERY_REQUIRED` rule ("after the
     named fee escrows") requires.
   - A backstop draw reruns `_assessAvailable` but does not reclassify again.
   - A check asserts that the reclassified Q equals `frozenFeeQ`.
2. **Keeper fees:** reclassified as one per-engine `keeperPoolQ` in the vault. The engine's
   `keeperQ[keeper]` still records who owns which part. A keeper's `withdrawKeeper()` moves their
   exact Q into their global `feeEscrowQ[keeper]` and pays its whole atoms.
   - Alternative: push every keeper's Q at reclassification. That needs an on-chain keeper list
     plus paging, because `keeperQ` is a mapping and cannot be enumerated.
3. **Where withdrawals go:** fee escrow withdrawals (`CollateralVault.withdrawFees()`, or the
   keeper path) credit the beneficiary's vault free balance. They do not go through
   `claimAtoms`/`claim`.
   - Effect: protocol fee withdrawals no longer run the engine's cash-claim fence (part of B-D04).
   - Reserve dust (`withdrawTreasury`) still goes through claim escrow, unchanged.
4. **Before settlement:** keeper withdrawals keep the old path: whole atoms leave the market
   allocation and the fraction stays in `keeperQ`. After reclassification, `_creditKeeper` reverts.
5. **Vault precision:** the vault keeps `marketDebitQ[engine]` < 1 atom. A market's allocation in Q
   is `marketAtoms * Q - marketDebitQ`.
   - `release`/`escrow` revert (`BadUnits`) if they would leave whole atoms below the debit.
   - `totalFeeEscrowQ` sums every fee escrow and keeper pool, so the custody identity can be
     checked.
6. **Protocol fee beneficiary:** the engine's existing immutable `treasury`. No new
   beneficiary-setting authority was added.

## API changes

- **Removed:** engine `protocolFeeEscrowQ()` and `withdrawProtocolFees()`.
- **Added on the engine:** `feesReclassified()`, `reclassifiedProtocolFeeQ()`, `reclassifiedKeeperQ()`,
  and the event `FeesReclassified`.
- **Added on the vault:**
  - functions: `reclassifyFees`, `assignKeeperFee`, `withdrawFees`
  - views: `marketDebitQ`, `feeEscrowQ`, `keeperPoolQ`, `totalFeeEscrowQ`
  - events: `FeesReclassified`, `KeeperFeeAssigned`, `FeesPaid`
- ABIs were re-exported (`artifacts/risk/engine-abi.json`, `vault-abi.json`). `docs/risk/HANDOFF.md`
  is updated. The SDK reads `MarketBalance`, whose fields are unchanged; after reclassification its
  fee fields read zero.

## Evidence

- `contracts/test/integration/AI01FeeEscrow.t.sol`:
  - 5 vault tests with a scripted engine double: exact debit, no payout of the reclassified
    fraction, floor withdrawal with cross-market aggregation, bounded keeper assignment, and access
    checks.
  - 2 tests on the real A+B engine: fees reclassified before the residual and untouched by every
    other exit, with the 0.6-atom fraction retained.
- These existing tests were updated to the new classification, with assertions kept exact:
  - `test/gates/G6.t.sol`
  - `test/audit/findings/AF03ProtocolFeeEscrow.t.sol`
  - `test/integration/A/CustodyExit.t.sol` (the keeper's half atom survives every other exit)
  - `test/risk/A/A038.t.sol`
  - `test/integration/EndToEnd.t.sol` (custody identity in Q)
  - the market-atoms identity in the invariant helpers (`marketAtoms * Q - marketDebitQ ==
    allocationQ`)
